Selected work

Redesigning class booking at CityFitness

I tested the CityFitness booking flow with five gym members. Black Card members thought that if they could book a class, they could attend it. A Premium member looked for yoga under Group Class and couldn’t find it there. In my redesign, members can see which classes their plan covers and find yoga and Pilates alongside other group classes.

Testing covered the original booking flow · The redesign hasn’t been retested yet

Helping families change plans at Te Papa

At a Wellington design make-a-thon, I explored what a family could do when the kids got tired halfway through a museum visit. I designed a route families could adjust as they went.

About

I’m a product designer in Wellington. I trained in visual design first, with a BFA in Design and Visual Communications and an MFA, and I’m now a PhD researcher in Museum and Heritage Studies at Victoria University of Wellington. My research looks at how visitors see and make sense of the objects in museum exhibitions. I asked a similar question while redesigning the CityFitness app: if a member thinks of yoga as a group class, where will they look for it? My art training shapes how I design, and my research shapes how I think about what people understand when they look at it.

I’m looking for Junior or Associate Product Designer roles, and I’m especially interested in B2B SaaS. Email me at pennywei595@gmail.com.
